9. Overseas Entity: Phased Approach

Phase 1 (Month 0-6): No Overseas Entity Needed

Use a Merchant of Record (MoR) service to sell globally from day one, without any overseas company:

MoR PlatformHow It WorksCost
PaddleActs as your legal seller. Handles global tax (VAT/GST), invoicing, refunds, compliance. You just build the product.5% + $0.50 per transaction
LemonSqueezySame model, more indie-friendly.5% + $0.50
Stripe (direct)You're the seller. Works fine but you handle tax compliance yourself.2.9% + $0.30

The MoR is your "virtual overseas entity" — Paddle/LemonSqueezy is the legal seller to the customer, collects payment, handles VAT/GST in 100+ countries, and remits to your Chinese bank account. Zero legal setup required.

Precedent: Many Chinese-founded global SaaS products (indie tools, AI apps) operate this way until they reach meaningful revenue.

Phase 2: When to Set Up an Overseas Entity

TriggerWhy

Revenue exceeds ~$500K/yearTax optimization becomes meaningful
