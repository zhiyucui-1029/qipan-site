# sportswear image prompts

## Cover Hero Prompt v0.1

用途：增长问题破局报告封面右侧行业图。

```text
Create a premium 16:9 consulting report cover visual for the sportswear and sports retail industry. The right-side hero image should show a close-up of a runner's foot in a premium running shoe stepping near a modern urban flagship store or glass commercial building. Use deep emerald green, dark navy, and muted gold. The image should be sophisticated, slightly cinematic, and low-key, with visible texture and depth but not too much contrast. Avoid exact official brand logos or trademark marks. The image is a background-like industry atmosphere layer, not an advertisement.
```

## 使用原则

- 封面图要服务标题，不要抢标题。
- 可以有跑鞋、城市、旗舰店、玻璃反光和运动感。
- 不要让 logo、鞋面细节或人物脸部成为主体。
- 生成后只保留精选无字 hero image，放进 `background-images/`。
